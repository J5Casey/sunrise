# Licensing FAQ

Sunrise's code is under the [PolyForm Shield licence](LICENSE). This page explains it in plain words; it isn't
part of the licence, and where they differ, the licence decides.

## What's fine without asking

- **Playing**, and everything a player does with Sunrise.
- **Running servers**, including selling ranks, perks, cosmetics or anything else on your server.
- **Hosting**: renting out Sunrise servers or offering them in a hosting panel.
- **Mods**, resource packs, data packs and shader packs for Sunrise, under any licence, free or sold.
- **Videos, streams and guides** about Sunrise, monetized or not.
- **Changing Sunrise** for your own use or your server, and sharing those changes, as long as what you share
  isn't a competing product.

## What isn't allowed

Anything built from Sunrise's code that competes with Sunrise: another client, launcher or repack, a fork offered
as an alternative, or putting Sunrise's code into such a product, paid or free. This holds whatever licence that
product uses.

## What else to know

- **Code that came from Horizon** is also available under its original MIT licence
  ([LICENSES/MIT-Horizon.txt](LICENSES/MIT-Horizon.txt)). The licence above only restricts Sunrise's own code.
- **Contributing:** there is no CLA. Contributors keep their copyright and their code comes in under the same
  licence it goes out under; commits are signed off under the Developer Certificate of Origin
  ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#licence-and-dco)). Sunrise never relicenses contributors' code under paid,
  proprietary or any other terms.
- **When you share Sunrise**, pass on the licence (or its URL) and the `Required Notice:` line at the top of
  [LICENSE](LICENSE).
